Browser payroll OCR uses Tesseract.js 6.0.1 and tesseract.js-core (Apache-2.0),
with Hebrew and English tessdata_fast language models (Apache-2.0).
Upstream sources: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
Language checksums are recorded in models/manifest.json.
The runtime files are copied from locked npm packages by scripts/browser-ocr-assets.mjs.
No CDN is contacted while processing a document.
